# Support

Thanks for taking a look at the page builder. We share it because we think it is useful, and we are glad when people use it, learn from it or build on it.

It is part of **commercetools-labs**: reference code that is freely available under the [MIT licence](LICENSE), shared as-is. It is **not covered by commercetools support**, and we can't promise replies, fixes or a roadmap. Please plan around that: pin the version you use, and be ready to adapt the code to your needs.

## What you can do

- **Fork it and make it yours.** That is what the licence is for, and it is usually the fastest route.
- **Open an issue or a pull request.** We read them when we can and we appreciate them, even though we can't commit to a response time. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Ask the community** if you have a general question about commercetools, Merchant Center custom applications or Connect.
- **Use commercetools support for the platform itself.** If something is wrong with commercetools Composable Commerce, Merchant Center or Connect rather than with this code, that is the right place, and they can help.

Thanks for understanding, and happy building.
